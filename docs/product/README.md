# Product

Who this is for, what they told us, and what the numbers say. Four documents,
each answering one question a reader is entitled to ask:

| Document | Question |
|---|---|
| [`icp.md`](icp.md) | Who is the first user, by name of segment rather than "traders"? |
| [`user-research.md`](user-research.md) | What did they actually do when they were handed the app? |
| [`gtm.md`](gtm.md) | How do they find us, and what do we count as working? |
| [`traction.md`](traction.md) | What has happened in production, in numbers? |

## How these are written

- **A claim carries its evidence or says it has none.** A line that has not been
  measured yet says so and names what would measure it. An unmarked number is a
  number from production.
- **Observation before conclusion.** `user-research.md` records what a person
  did and said; what we changed because of it is a separate column, and only
  what two or more people hit gets changed at all.
- **Dated.** Every document carries the date it was last true. Product claims
  rot faster than code.
