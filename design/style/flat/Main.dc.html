Stage 2 · the two chosen directions
Ten screens, two skins
Every artboard below is a snapshot of the clickable prototype with the theme switched — same markup, same copy, same numbers. Top row Terminal, bottom row Paper; left to right follows the path: lobby, strategy, position, result, settings, lesson, leaderboard, history, risk, account.
Terminal
after Hyperliquid · Archivo
Near-black with one mint accent and monospaced numbers. The accent and the fill are the same colour, so the primary button IS the brand — a single loud thing per screen.
Costs: Unforgiving to a beginner. Mint doubles as "up", so a neutral highlight can read as profit.
Paper
after Monad · Space Grotesk
Off-white with Monad purple. The fill is near-black and the accent is purple, so buttons stay calm and the brand shows up only where something is live: the signal, the prize, a selected chip.
Costs: Worse at 2am and under a bar light. Purple carries no market meaning, so up and down need their own green and red.
Both rows are driven by one token block in design/path/tradeagent-proto.html. A colour changed there changes both the clickable prototype and this canvas — there is no second copy to keep in sync.
