GLOSSARY
Every word the app uses
One term, one sentence a stranger understands. Each appears in the app with this text behind a dashed underline.
Passkey
Your account key, made on this phone and unlocked by Face ID. No password to remember, no seed phrase to lose.
AUSD
The dollar you trade with. One AUSD is one dollar. On testnet it is practice money the exchange gives you.
Testnet
A practice copy of the exchange. Real prices, real mechanics, no real money. Everything you win or lose here is for training.
15x
Your 50 AUSD moves like 750. A 1% move in Bitcoin is 7.50 AUSD for you, up or down. The market's maximum by default; lower it in position settings.
Stop −50%
If the trade loses half of what you put in, we close it. That is the most you can lose on one tap.
15 minutes
How long the trade lives. It closes on its own when the time runs out, whatever the price. You never have to remember to close.
Take profit
Optional. Closes the trade when it has won the amount you set, before the time runs out. Off by default.
Fees
What the exchange charges to open and close: about 26 cents on a 50 AUSD tap at 15x. Already counted in the number you see.
Liquidation
The exchange closing a trade that lost everything you put in. Your stop fires long before that, so on this app you should never see it.
Strategy
A set of rules for when to trade. Direction: you decide. MA Cross and RSI Bounce: the screen tells you when, you tap once.
Prize pool
A weekly pot paid to the players with the best results in each strategy. Settled on-chain every Sunday; you collect it with one tap.
